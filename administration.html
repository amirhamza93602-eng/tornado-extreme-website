Leadership Directory

Official roles and responsibilities

EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP

Club President

Provides overall direction, represents the club and oversees its long-term vision.

FOUNDING LEADERSHIP

Club Founder

Established the club identity, culture and foundation on which the community grows.

CLUB OPERATIONS

General Secretary

Coordinates administration, official communication, records and club operations.

COMPETITIVE OPERATIONS

Team Captain

Leads players in competition, supports squad discipline and coordinates matchday activity.

ROSTER & DEVELOPMENT

Squad Management

Supports MAIN, ACADEMY, YOUTH and LEGEND squad organisation and player development.

DIGITAL OPERATIONS

Portal Administration

Maintains player verification, portal access, submissions and official digital records.
